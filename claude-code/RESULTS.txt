Evaluation loss is always MSE on hard voltages at those 3 neurons (regardless of
what loss was used during training). Lower loss and exact spike counts = better.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Open questions / next directions
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Why does case 2 not converge with spike-timing loss?
   - Soft+ST gets N49 correct but N47/N48 = 0 every time. Is there a local basin
     where N49 fires but N47/N48 are suppressed? More restarts may help.
2. Can we tune TAU? Larger tau (50–100) would broaden the attraction basin around
   each spike target and might help case 2.
3. Better initialization — greedy or activity-based weight assignment before
   gradient descent to avoid the zero-spike local minima.
4. Why does soft+ST overshoot on N48 in case 0 with MSE (Run 3: 5/2 spikes)?
   The spike-timing loss fixes this; understanding why may give insight into the
   loss landscape structure.
